## The problem

Before this, I hadn't done a CAD project with real curved, swept geometry — everything up to this point was boxy. A 1:1 replica of an actual mini bike chassis, matched to real wheels and a real motor, forced learning surface modeling instead of extrudes.

## The build

A 1:1 replica of a mini bike chassis, dimensioned to fit real mini bike wheels and a Honda motor, modeled entirely in CAD using surface modeling and sweep features for the frame's curved tubing.
