## The problem

Standard prosthetic feet are passive — they don't actively correct for uneven terrain or shifting balance the way a natural foot and ankle do, which leaves users compensating with the rest of their body.

## The build

StablStep actively balances across multiple axes, something standard prosthetics can't do. A PID control loop handles real-time stabilization, while a machine learning layer predicts the wearer's steps and adjusts the foot's position in advance rather than only reacting after the fact. The mechanism combines 3D-printed structure with aluminum for the load-bearing components, driven by an Arduino-based control system.
