## The problem

Project-based robotics programs need curriculum that a teacher without a robotics background can actually run — not just a parts kit, but slides, sequencing, and teaching notes that hold up in a real classroom.

## The build

A complete hands-on curriculum built around an ESP-driven sensor car: students wire up DC motors and sensors, program the ESP, and get a working car by the end of it. It ships with full slideshows and teaching notes so any instructor can pick it up and run it without needing to design the lesson flow themselves.
